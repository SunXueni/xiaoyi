Core Pain Point Analysis

The meniscus has limited self-healing capacity, improper management or overuse after injury can accelerate joint degeneration. Patients face a difficult dilemma: resting too much leads to muscle atrophy, while normal activity may worsen joint pressure. Existing solutions are either passive fixation or overly restrictive, unable to adapt to users' real-time activity level changes.

Intelligent Dynamic Knee Rehabilitation Solution

Our innovation aims to fill the gap of dynamic adaptive support systems, achieving real-time joint pressure sharing and adjustment through intelligent knee support scaffold

Vertical Load Offloading

Through integrated elastic components, storing elastic potential energy during leg bending, releasing lifting force during extension and standing, reducing knee joint compressive stress

Controlled Valgus Moment

Introducing mild controlled valgus moment, fine-tuning knee alignment during movement, regulating load distribution across tibiofemoral joint, reducing shear and compressive forces

Intelligent Sensing & Feedback

Integrating IMU, tension/strain/thin film pressure sensors with ESP32 main control, real-time monitoring of knee angle and force, providing rehabilitation data quantification

Personalized Customization

Based on 3D printing technology, achieving personalized customization of structural parameters such as stiffness, lift magnitude, torque, adapting to different body types and rehabilitation stages

Core Technology: Biomechanics & Intelligent Engineering Integration

Three-Chamber Unloading Biomechanical Structure

Simulating the load-sharing and shock-absorbing functions of the natural meniscus, rather than merely restricting movement like traditional knee braces. Core mechanical configuration combines elastic load offloading, valgus force application, and low-friction joint structure.

Elastic Energy Storage & Release

During leg bending, elastic components deform and store elastic potential energy; during extension and standing, release energy as lifting force. This passive adaptive mechanism is both energy-efficient and reliable.

3D Printing Customized Production

Using additive manufacturing technology, achieving cost-effective production and customization. Incorporating bearings at joint rotation points significantly reduces friction, ensuring smooth, low-resistance movement.

Intelligent Rehabilitation Monitoring System

Supporting mobile APP provides real-time feedback and personalized rehabilitation plans, achieving home-use rehabilitation data monitoring, connecting users with rehabilitation professionals.
